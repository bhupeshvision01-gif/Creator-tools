Creator Economy Calculators

YouTube Income Calculator

Estimate your potential YouTube earnings based on monthly views and CPM rates.

Formula: Income = (Views / 1000) × CPM
Example: 100,000 views × $2 CPM → $200

Instagram Engagement Calculator

Calculate your Instagram engagement rate based on likes, comments, and followers.

Formula: Engagement % = ((Likes + Comments)/Followers) × 100
Example: 500 likes + 50 comments / 10,000 followers → 5.5%

TikTok Earnings Calculator

Estimate TikTok Creator Fund earnings based on video views.

Formula: Earnings = (Views / 1000) × CPM
Example: 50,000 views × $1.5 CPM → $75

CPM Calculator

Calculate Cost Per Mille (cost per 1000 impressions) for your ad campaigns.

Formula: CPM = (Total Cost / Impressions) × 1000
Example: $200 / 50,000 impressions → $4 CPM

Affiliate Income Calculator

Calculate potential affiliate marketing earnings from clicks and conversions.

Formula: Income = Clicks × (Conversion % / 100) × Commission
Example: 500 clicks × 5% × $10 → $250

Brand Deal Calculator

Estimate your sponsored post rates based on follower count.

Formula: Brand Deal = Rate per Post
Example: $500 per post → $500

Creator ROI Calculator

Calculate return on investment for your content creation efforts.

Formula: ROI % = ((Revenue − Cost) / Cost) × 100
Example: $1,500 revenue − $500 cost → 200%

Subscriber Growth Calculator

Project subscriber growth over time with compound growth rates.

Formula: Subscribers after Months = Current + (Monthly Gain × Months)
Example: 1,000 current + 200/month × 6 months → 2,200

Video Profit Calculator

Calculate net profit from video production costs and monetization.

Formula: Profit = Revenue − Cost
Example: $1,000 revenue − $400 cost → $600

Influencer Rate Calculator

Calculate ideal rates for sponsored content based on platform and engagement.

Formula: Rate = Followers × Engagement % × $0.05
Example: 10,000 followers × 5% × $0.05 → $25
