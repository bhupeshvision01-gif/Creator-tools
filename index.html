Finance Calculators

Loan EMI Calculator

Formula: EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n −1]
Example: $10,000, 8% interest, 2 years → $452/month

Compound Interest Calculator

Formula: A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t)
Example: $5,000, 5% rate, 4 times/year, 3 years → $5,796.38

Simple Interest Calculator

Formula: SI = P × R × T / 100
Example: $1,000 × 5% × 3 years → $150
