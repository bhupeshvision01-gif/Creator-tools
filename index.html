Creator Economy Calculators

All monetary calculations are in USD ($)

Creator Economy Calculators

YouTube Income Calculator

Formula: Income = (Views / 1000) × CPM
Example: 100,000 views × $2 CPM → $200

TikTok Earnings Calculator

Formula: Earnings = (Views / 1000) × CPM
Example: 50,000 views × $1.5 CPM → $75

Affiliate Income Calculator

Formula: Income = Clicks × (Conversion % / 100) × Commission
Example: 500 clicks × 5% × $10 → $250

Brand Deal Calculator

Formula: Brand Deal = Rate per Post
Example: $500 per post → $500

Video Profit Calculator

Formula: Profit = Revenue − Cost
Example: $300 revenue − $100 cost → $200

Influencer Rate Calculator

Formula: Rate = Followers × (Engagement % / 100) × 0.1
Example: 10,000 followers × 5% → $50

Instagram Engagement Calculator

Formula: Engagement % = ((Likes + Comments) / Followers) × 100
Example: 200 likes + 20 comments / 5,000 followers → 4.4%

Creator ROI Calculator

Formula: ROI % = ((Revenue − Cost) / Cost) × 100
Example: $500 revenue − $200 cost → 150%

Subscriber Growth Calculator

Formula: Subscribers after Months = Current + (Monthly Gain × Months)
Example: 1,000 current + 200/month × 6 months → 2,200

CPM Calculator

Formula: CPM = (Revenue / Views) × 1000
Example: $200 revenue / 100,000 views → $2 CPM
