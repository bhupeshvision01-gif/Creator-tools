Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good YouTube CPM?

CPM ranges from $2-12. Finance/tech niches average $8-12, gaming/entertainment $2-5.

How much does YouTube pay for 1 million views?

Average $2,000-12,000 depending on CPM, audience location, and ad engagement.

Do all views count toward earnings?

No, only ~55% of views are typically monetized due to ad blockers and skip rates.
