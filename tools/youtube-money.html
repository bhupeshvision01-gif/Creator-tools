❓ Frequently Asked Questions

💰 What is a good YouTube CPM in 2024?

CPM ranges $2-15. Top niches: Finance ($8-15), Tech ($6-12), Gaming ($1.50-4), Beauty ($4-8).

📊 How much does 1M views pay?

$1,100-8,250 (after 55% monetization & YouTube's 45% cut). Average: ~$3,500.

⚠️ Why only 55% monetization?

Ad blockers, view skips, unmonetizable content, and geographic restrictions reduce monetized views.

⏰ When do I get paid?

Monthly via AdSense (21st-26th) if you reach $100 threshold and have valid PayPal/bank.
